Evaluation breakdown

BuildingBench grades each submitted glTF against held-out images, point clouds, and the file itself. Scores are normalized so 0 is the building's bounding-box baseline and 1 is perfect.

Reliable auto-verification 85%+ correlation with human expertsWe asked human experts with 3D expertise to evaluate the Building Arena by selecting their preferred reconstruction from pairs generated by different models. Their preferences agreed with metric-based evaluations for around 85% of the pairs.
OverallThe benchmark's normalized measurements receive equal shares. Equal weighting performs as well as weights fitted to held-out human preferences.
Surface agreementPrecision, recall, and F at a 0.5 m tolerance, plus Chamfer distance between the submission and truth point clouds.
GeometrySilhouette 45% · outline 20% · plan 20% · height 15%, measured from held-out camera views.
AppearanceColour 40% · façade structure 30% · materials 30%. Materials are read directly from the submitted glTF.
Held-out consistencyThe agent receives 4 photographs and camera poses; 4 held-out cameras test whether the reconstruction works beyond the observed views.
Reliability & effortAcross-building SD, repeat SD, cost, turns, and runtime are reported separately. The measured overall noise floor is 0.05–0.09.
