How the ranking is measured
One number per model: the mean of its overall over the same 12 license-clean buildings (CC BY 4.0, from the Helsinki 3D city model and the City of Melbourne 2020 photomesh), one run per building, so every row is measured on identical cases and the rows are directly comparable. The bar chart shows that number out of 100. The ± is that model’s own standard error: the deviation of its score from one building to the next, over the root of twelve. Cost / run is the median of what one run cost, as the agent’s CLI reported it; rows tagged est. price are priced from their recorded tokens at the published list prices instead, and SWE-2’s estimate is $0.00, which is why it sits in a lane of its own on the Pareto frontier. frontier marks the rows on that frontier: nothing cheaper scores higher. The # column is the position in the current sort, nothing more — neighbouring rows are often closer than their ± can separate. Board snapshot 2026-09-22.
